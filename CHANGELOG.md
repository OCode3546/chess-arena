# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- First stable release: play the computer, a friend on the same computer, or anyone online.
- Online play is tested end to end on every release: two apps play by invite code and quick match, and a player who quits mid-game rejoins.
- Clear message in the app when the game server is in maintenance; games in progress can still be finished.
- Security updates for bundled libraries.

## 0.9.0

- Play online: quick match (3+2, 5+3, 10+0, 15+10) or a private game with a 6-character invite code. No account needed.
- The game server decides every move, clock and result; draw offers, abort, resign and rematch offers.
- Automatic reconnect with clock resync; a disconnected player has 60 s to return. Rejoin a live game after quitting from Home.
- Online XP (80 / 35 / 12 with bonuses), online stats on the profile and online games in History.
- Settings: the display name opponents see and whether to show rematch offers.

## 0.8.0

- Downloadable installers for Windows (installer + portable exe) and macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel).
- Automatic updates on Windows; an "update available" banner and download link on Mac and the portable exe.
- Native macOS window buttons and app menu (About, Check for Updates, Settings with Cmd+,, Quit with Cmd+Q).
- Keyboard hints and wording adapt to Windows or Mac.

## 0.7.0

- Game history with move-by-move review and PGN export.
- Sound effects for moves, captures, checks, castling, promotion, results and level-ups.
- Settings: sound and volume, launch at startup, data export/import, reset progress, data folder.
- How to Play guide with rules, diagrams and XP explained.
- About: credits, bundled license texts and source links.

## 0.6.0

- Rewards roadmap for levels 1–50 with equippable boards, pieces, avatars, titles, frames and effects.
- Achievements with unlock toasts.

## 0.5.0

- Local profiles, XP and levels up to 50 with a full XP breakdown after each game.

## 0.4.0

- Play against Stockfish at six difficulties, with WebAssembly and built-in fallback engines.
- Hints, takebacks and draw offers against the computer.

## 0.3.0

- Local two-player games with clocks, pause, resign and draw offers.
- Auto-save and Continue Game.
